
# Citation Review Notes

Verified source classes used in the manuscript:

- TNSRE scope and author constraints: official IEEE/EMBS TNSRE submission guidelines.
- IEEE/Overleaf formatting: official IEEE Author Center / Overleaf IEEE template pages.
- DB10/MeganePro: Scientific Data data descriptor by Cognolato et al. (2020), DOI 10.1038/s41597-020-0380-3.
- DB10 contextual comparators: Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence (Cognolato et al., 2022) and Frontiers in Robotics and AI (Wang et al., 2022).
- Hyser: IEEE TNSRE paper by Jiang et al. (2021), DOI 10.1109/TNSRE.2021.3082551, and PhysioNet v2.0.0, DOI 10.13026/hxan-pe94.

- GRABMyo: Scientific Data article by Pradhan, He, and Jiang (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41597-022-01836-y, and PhysioNet v1.1.0, DOI 10.13026/89dm-f662.

- Calibration: Guo et al., PMLR 70:1321--1330 (2017).
- Myoelectric selective classification and robustness: Scheme et al. TBME 2011; Hargrove et al. EMBC 2006; Jiang et al. IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2012.
